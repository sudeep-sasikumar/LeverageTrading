Training history

Grinders are coins that move steadily one way for hours (straight path, most hours the same way, no single candle doing the work). Every night LTS re-studies the last ~4 months: it finds every steady grind, marks the moments you could have entered, and compares the ones that kept going with lookalikes that fizzled. Every hour it checks which coins are grinding now and how many of those traits they have. The best are paper-traded (−3% stop, +6% target, 24h).
